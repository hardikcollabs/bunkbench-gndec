# BunkBench GNDEC

### Notes, chaos, and semester survival.

**[Open BunkBench GNDEC](https://hardikcollabs.github.io/bunkbench-gndec/)**

BunkBench GNDEC is a student-made space for Mechanical Engineering students at GNDEC - a place to find the things that should not require searching through forty-seven WhatsApp messages.

## Why this exists

College life is already busy enough. Notes get scattered, syllabus links disappear inside chats, timetables are hard to read on a phone, and someone is always asking where the PDF is.

BunkBench is built to make that a little easier: one clean, useful, slightly chaotic place for resources that students actually need.

## What is on the bench

- Semester-wise syllabus and resources
- Notes, PYQs, lab material, and useful links
- Readable MEA and MEB timetables
- Student updates with links back to official sources
- A positive Mechanical Engineering Meme Pit

## The vision

It starts with GNDEC Mechanical Engineering, Semester 3. The long-term idea is simple: make student resources easier to find, easier to understand, and less stressful to use - campus by campus.

## Built by

Built and maintained independently by a GNDEC student.

BunkBench GNDEC is a small, independent attempt to make Mechanical Engineering resources easier to find and less stressful to use.

## Important

BunkBench GNDEC is an unofficial student-made resource hub. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by GNDEC. Always verify official notices, dates, and timetable changes through official college channels.
