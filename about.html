Who put this bench here?

Built by Hardik.
For the bench.

BunkBench GNDEC is independently created and maintained by Hardik Kumar. Hardik is the student creator behind this small, student-first project: one student, one laptop, and a genuine dislike for searching through 47 WhatsApp messages just to find one PDF.

College life already has enough friction: scattered notes, syllabus links buried in chats, unclear timetables, last-minute resource hunting, and juniors repeatedly asking seniors where everything is. Useful stuff exists - it is just rarely in one place.

BunkBench is my attempt to fix that, one useful link at a time. It is a student-first home for notes, syllabus, PYQs, lab manuals, readable timetables, helpful updates, and positive Mechanical Engineering memes. No fake official vibe. No unnecessary drama. Just things that make student life a little less chaotic.

The project starts with GNDEC Mechanical Engineering, Semester 3. If it helps even one student spend less time hunting and more time actually understanding, it is doing its job.

For now, the bench belongs to GNDEC Mechanical Engineering. If the format proves useful, it can grow carefully to more semesters, departments, and campuses - with the same rule: real student resources first, no made-up academic details.

Important: BunkBench is an unofficial, independent student-made resource hub. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by GNDEC. Always verify official notices and timetable changes through the college's official channels.
